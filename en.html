Plump Card — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-13
Developer: Hsuan Yu Liu
Contact: lovesnoopy.world@gmail.com

Summary

Plump Card has no server of its own and no Plump Card account. When you do not use AI, sharing, exporting, or another external service, the business cards you scan, the contact details recognized from them, and the images stay on your iPhone. Plump Card does not operate analytics or behavioral tracking; AdMob, AI, sharing, and export features process data as described below.

1. Where your data lives

  • Structured card fields, recognized text, images, meeting context, tags, your digital card: in the iPhone app sandbox, encrypted with iOS file protection.
  • The Release version does not provide external address-book export, so no external-service authorization token is created.
  • AI provider API key (if you enable AI recognition): iOS Keychain only, marked device-only — not synced to iCloud, not restored to another device.
  • Plump Card does not upload any of the above to our servers — we have none.

2. What we process, why, and on what basis

DataPurposeBasis
Card image and OCR textRecognize and organize into a contactYour request
Contact fields (name, company, title, department, phone, email, address, social…)Core card-library functionYour request
Meeting context, tags, notesRelationship managementYour request
Local action log (action type + time only, no field content, kept 90 days)Audit / troubleshootingLegitimate interest (minimized)

We do not independently collect diagnostics / crash / behavioral data, location, or personal data beyond contacts. There is no third-party analytics SDK in the app; the free-tier AdMob SDK may process device, advertising, and interaction data under Google's policy. See §3A.

Ads (free tier, non-Pro): If you have not purchased Pro, the app may show Google AdMob Rewarded ads before saving a new card after the first 50 cards. The ad must be completed before saving; cancellation or no-fill does not save the card. Google processes ad-related data under its policies; this may include device identifiers, advertising IDs, and IP. Pro removes ads permanently. See §3A below.

3. AI card recognition (optional)

Recognition runs entirely on your iPhone by default; card content does not leave the device.

In Settings › Card recognition you can switch to AI or Hybrid mode. Before enabling it, a one-time consent screen explains:

  • The card's image and recognized text will be sent to the third-party AI provider you choose (currently Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google), which processes it under its own privacy policy.
  • It is sent using your own API key; any costs are between you and that provider.
  • Plump Card has no server involved in this — we do not intermediate and do not store your key, queries, or results. Retention, deletion, and other processing are governed by the provider you choose; Plump Card does not control its retention period.
  • Hybrid mode calls the AI only when on-device recognition is not confident.
  • You can switch back to on-device recognition at any time; card content then stops leaving the device.
  • Before using AI recognition, make sure you are allowed to process the card holder's personal data.

The app shows a rough local count of AI recognitions / tokens this month for your reference only; actual usage and cost are per your provider's bill. This count never leaves your device.

3A. Free-tier ads (Google AdMob)

  • Who sees them: Users who have not purchased Plump Card Pro and are past the lifetime first-50-cards ad-free trial.
  • When: Before saving a new card after recognition and confirmation. The Rewarded ad must be completed; it never blocks capture or editing.
  • Frequency: Eligibility starts with card 51; the production cooldown is at least 2 minutes and there is no catch-up display.
  • Data: Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may use device advertising IDs, coarse location (IP), and app interaction signals per Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob policies. We do not receive your card content for ads.
  • Control: Pro removes ads permanently; iOS Privacy & Tracking settings may affect personalization.
  • Trial count: The 50-card trial is stored on-device in Keychain on a best-effort basis (not guaranteed across devices).

Third-party links:

  • OpenAI Privacy Policy: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Exporting to external address books

When you choose Add to system Contacts, you write selected cards one-way into the Contacts app on your iPhone. You always preview first. Plump Card does not import from system Contacts or automatically sync in both directions. If you share a CSV or vCard, or share to LINE, WhatsApp, or another app, the data is sent to the recipient you choose.

Copies written to system Contacts or shared with another app or service are not automatically deleted when you delete data in Plump Card; delete them through the receiving app or service according to its features and policy.

The Release version currently does not provide export to Google, Outlook, or other external address books.

5. Your rights and controls

  • Sharing: The Release version provides Share CSV and Share vCard; full-backup sharing, export, import, and restore are not supported. A shared CSV contains card contact details; keep it safe.
  • Delete (four operations, each labeled with what it affects):
    1. Clear all data — deletes everything on the iPhone (library, images, recognized text, merge-undo records, your digital card, external OAuth tokens, AI keys and consent record).
    2. The Release version does not create or manage external address-book authorization tokens. Copies shared with other apps or written to system Contacts must be removed through those apps.
    3. (from 1.5) Delete iCloud sync data.
    4. (future AI-account feature) Delete AI account and research data — not yet shipped.
  • Correct: edit any field directly in the app.
  • Because data is primarily on your device and there is no Plump Card account, we cannot access or delete it for you. Requests concerning data shared with a third party must be made to that third party; local actions are completed in the app.

6. Permissions

  • Camera: to photograph cards.
  • Photos: to import card photos from your library.
  • Contacts: to write organized contacts after you confirm.

7. Retention

Data is kept until you delete it. Export job records are cleared 30 days after success; the local action log is cleared after 90 days. The app runs these on launch.

8. Children

Plump Card is designed for business users, is not directed to children under 13, and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

9. Third parties

  • Apple (Sign in with Apple, CloudKit — from 1.5): identity and sync data in your own iCloud, under Apple's terms.
  • Anthropic / OpenAI / Google (only when you enable AI recognition, with your own key): see §3.
  • Google AdMob (free tier only, when §3A applies): see §3A.
  • Names and trademarks belong to their respective owners; this app refers to them nominatively to describe functionality, with no affiliation or endorsement.

10. Changes

We update the effective date on this page when the policy changes; material changes are surfaced in the app.
